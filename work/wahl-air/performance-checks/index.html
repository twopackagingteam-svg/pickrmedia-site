Performance checks that keep your system at its best.

A performance check ensures your air conditioning system is operating efficiently, safely, and at full capacity. We test key components and system output to catch hidden issues before they turn into breakdowns.

Signs You Need a Performance Check

When output
slips

  • AC is running but cooling feels weaker than usual
  • Rising energy bills without changes in usage
  • System runs longer cycles to cool the home
  • Uneven temperatures between rooms
  • AC struggles during hot afternoons
  • No recent professional system evaluation
Measured, not guessed
What We Evaluate

Five measurements
in every check

01
Airflow performance

We measure airflow to ensure your system is moving air properly through your home.

02
Temperature output

We check supply and return air temperatures to confirm proper cooling performance.

03
Refrigerant operation

We evaluate system pressures and performance to ensure correct cooling efficiency.

04
Electrical performance

We test capacitors, motors, and controls for proper and safe operation.

05
Overall system efficiency

We assess how effectively your system is cooling relative to energy use.
