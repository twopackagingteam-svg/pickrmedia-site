Questions We Hear Every Day

Straight answers

Q

How do I know what size AC my house needs?

We calculate it — square footage, insulation, window exposure, ceiling height and ductwork all factor in. Wrong-sized systems waste energy or fail to keep up; ours are load-calculated.

Q

How long does an installation take?

Most residential replacements are done in a day. One recent review: both units replaced, started early morning, complete by midday.

Q

Can I finance a new system?

Yes — GreenSky plans including 6-month and 15-month no-interest promotional options and 120-month fixed-rate terms, subject to credit approval.

If a Carrier unit we install fails within its first 12 months, we replace the unit completely — not just repair it.
