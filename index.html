Questions

Straight answers.

Is the preview really free?

Yes. We build the whole site before asking for anything. If you don't want it, reply "no thanks" and we take the preview down. You will not get an invoice.

You emailed me a website I never asked for. What is this?

That's how we work. We find established local businesses whose web presence doesn't match the quality of their work, build a site from their public details, and send the owner a private link. The preview is unlisted and hidden from search engines. If you'd rather we remove it, reply to the email and it's gone the same day, and we won't contact you again.

Where does the information on my preview come from?

Only from what your business has already made public: your Google listing, your social pages, your current website. We don't add claims you haven't made yourself. If anything is out of date, tell us and we'll correct it.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. If you have one, we connect it. If you don't, we'll help you register one in your own name so you always control it. Domain registration is typically around $15 to $20 a year, paid directly to the registrar.

What counts as an update?

Changes to text, hours, phone numbers, photos, service areas, and adding or removing a service. New features such as online booking or a store are quoted separately, before any work starts.

What happens if I cancel?

On Pay monthly, the site comes down at the end of the paid month; after 12 paid months it's yours to keep. On Own it, you can stop hosting anytime and we send you your site files.

How do we talk?

By email, at hello@pickrmedia.com. It keeps a written record of every request and lets us turn changes around quickly.
