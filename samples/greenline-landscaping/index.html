03About

About Greenline Landscaping

Greenline Landscaping has been designing and looking after gardens in Brookhaven Mills since 1998. The company began with lawn care and has grown into a full design, build and maintenance practice with its own nursery yard on Orchard Lane.

Projects range from a single planting bed to complete gardens with stonework, irrigation and lighting, and many clients stay on for seasonal maintenance once the build is finished.

05FAQ

Common questions

General answers about how landscaping work is done. For anything specific to your job, call (555) 016-3391.

What does a landscape design include?

A plan of the space with layout, planting scheme and materials, so the work can be priced and built in stages.

When is the best time to plant?

Spring and autumn are the usual planting seasons, when soil is workable and heat stress is low.

Do you maintain what you build?

Seasonal maintenance is listed as a service where it is offered — ask when you request a quote.

How do I get a quote?

Call (555) 016-3391 or send the form on this page with a few details about the job — what needs doing, where, and roughly when. If the work needs a look in person, that gets arranged from there.

Which areas do you cover?

The places listed in the service area section. If your address is near one of them but not on the list, call and ask.

Do I need to be there while the work is done?

That depends on the job and on access. Say what suits you when you request a quote and it can be worked out in advance.
