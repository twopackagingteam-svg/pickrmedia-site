05FAQ

Common questions

General answers about how heating & cooling work is done. For anything specific to your job, call (555) 014-7782.

Should I repair or replace my air conditioner or furnace?

A single failed part on a system in otherwise good condition is usually worth repairing. Frequent breakdowns, rising running costs or a unit near the end of its life point towards replacement.

How often should heating and cooling equipment be serviced?

A check before each season — cooling in spring, heating in autumn — is the common schedule.

What size system does my home need?

Sizing is based on the building: its area, insulation, windows and layout, not just the old unit. An installer measures this before quoting.

How do I get a quote?

Call (555) 014-7782 or send the form on this page with a few details about the job — what needs doing, where, and roughly when. If the work needs a look in person, that gets arranged from there.

Which areas do you cover?

The places listed in the service area section. If your address is near one of them but not on the list, call and ask.

Do I need to be there while the work is done?

That depends on the job and on access. Say what suits you when you request a quote and it can be worked out in advance.
