About Summit Plumbing

Summit Plumbing was started in 2009 by two journeyman plumbers working out of a single van. Today the company runs a crew of service plumbers from its shop on Granite Street, covering Pinecrest Valley and the towns along the foothills.

05FAQ

Common questions

General answers about how plumbing work is done. For anything specific to your job, call (555) 013-2040.

What should I do about a leak before the plumber arrives?

Turn off the nearest shut-off valve or the main water supply, and keep water away from electrical outlets. Then call.

Should a water heater be repaired or replaced?

Repairs make sense for isolated faults such as an element, thermostat or valve. A tank that is leaking from the body, or a unit well past its expected life, is usually replaced.

What causes recurring drain clogs?

Grease, hair, wipes and tree roots are the usual causes. A camera inspection shows whether the problem is in the fixture, the branch line or the main sewer.

How do I get a quote?

Call (555) 013-2040 or send the form on this page with a few details about the job — what needs doing, where, and roughly when. If the work needs a look in person, that gets arranged from there.

Which areas do you cover?

Do I need to be there while the work is done?

That depends on the job and on access. Say what suits you when you request a quote and it can be worked out in advance.
