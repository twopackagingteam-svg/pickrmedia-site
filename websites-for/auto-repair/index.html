What it has to do

What a website for auto repair shops actually needs.

Address, hours and a map link first

A shop is a place. Your address, a one-tap directions link and today's hours are at the top, because that's what people look for first.

What you work on

Makes you service, and the work you do: brakes, diagnostics, AC, transmissions, inspections, tires. Listed plainly so nobody has to call to ask.

Honest, plain language

No jargon and no hype. Drivers are wary; a site that reads straight builds more trust than one that shouts.

Call or request an appointment

A tap-to-call button and a short appointment request form, both easy to use one-handed.

What's on it

Built from your real details. Nothing invented.

We build from what your business already publishes: your Google listing, your social pages, your current site. Your logo, your services, your words. If a fact isn't yours, it isn't on the page.

Questions from auto repair shops

Straight answers.

Can the site show ASE certification or a warranty on repairs?

If you already advertise it, yes. We don't add certifications, warranties or price promises that your shop hasn't made.

Can you list our prices?

Only prices you already publish or send us. Many shops prefer a request form instead, and that's what the site uses by default.
