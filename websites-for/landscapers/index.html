What it has to do

What a website for landscapers actually needs.

A design that feels like your work

Warm, considered and uncluttered. The site should feel like a well-kept garden, not a coupon flyer.

Maintenance and projects are different customers

Weekly mowing is a subscription decision; a patio or planting design is a project. The services you offer are grouped so each visitor finds their path.

Your photos, given room

If you've shared photos of your work publicly, they're the star of the page. We never use stock garden photos to stand in for your work.

Seasonal services stay visible

Spring cleanups, aeration, leaf removal, snow. Listed by name so they're found when the season turns.

What's on it

Built from your real details. Nothing invented.

We build from what your business already publishes: your Google listing, your social pages, your current site. Your logo, your services, your words. If a fact isn't yours, it isn't on the page.
