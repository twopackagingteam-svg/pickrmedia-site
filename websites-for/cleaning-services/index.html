Questions from cleaning companies

Straight answers.

Can customers book and pay online?

The standard site has a quote request form. Online booking and payment can be added. We'd quote that separately before building it.

We're just two people. Will the site make that obvious?

The site describes your services and how to reach you. It doesn't state your headcount unless you want it to, and it never pretends you're something you're not.
