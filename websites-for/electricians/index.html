What it has to do

What a website for electricians actually needs.

Licensing up front

If you display your license number today, it goes near the top of the site. For electrical work it's the first thing careful customers look for.

Residential and commercial, clearly split

Panel upgrades, EV chargers, generators, rewiring, lighting, tenant build-outs. Customers should see in seconds whether you do their kind of job.

Newer work that people search by name

EV charger installation and whole-home generators are searched constantly and by name. If you do them, they get their own clear entry.

A quote form that asks the right thing

A short form that captures the job and the address, so you can call back prepared instead of playing phone tag.
