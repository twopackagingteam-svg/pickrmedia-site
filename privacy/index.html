Privacy Policy

Last updated September 20, 2026

This policy explains what Pickr Media ("Pickr Media") collects, why, and what you can do about it. We keep this short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • What you send us: the business name, link, your name, email address and any notes you enter in our request form, plus anything you email us.
  • Public business information: when we build a preview for a business, we use details that business has published about itself (name, logo, phone, address, hours, services, public reviews and photos on its own pages). This is business information, not information about private individuals.
  • Payment details: handled entirely by our payment processor, Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • Basic technical logs: our hosting provider records standard request data (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the service secure and working.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't run advertising trackers on this site.
  • We don't add you to a marketing list because you asked for a preview.

How we use it

To build and send previews, reply to you, provide and bill for our service, keep the site secure, and meet legal obligations.

Outreach emails

If we email a business about a preview we built, the email says who we are, includes our postal address, and gives a simple way to opt out. Opt-outs are honored promptly and permanently.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers we need to operate: our hosting provider, our email provider and Stripe for payments. Each handles data under its own security and privacy commitments. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting

Request-form details for previews you don't buy are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept for as long as you're a client and for as long afterwards as tax and accounting rules require. You can ask us to delete or correct your information at any time.

Your choices

Email hello@pickrmedia.com to access, correct or delete your information, to have a preview removed, or to stop all contact. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy law, and we will honor them.

Children

This site is for businesses. It isn't directed to children and we don't knowingly collect their information.
