Why the crane

Some trees can't be dropped. They have to be lifted.

Over a roof. Over the power lines. Leaning toward the lake. When there's no room to fell a tree, a crane lifts it out in controlled pieces — cleanly, from above, without tearing up the yard to do it.

Tennessee Tree & Crane Service is a full-service tree and crane company serving the greater Cleveland, TN area, with 15+ years in business. Crane, bucket truck, Bobcat and hauling arrive as one crew, so the job gets finished — not handed off.

  • Truck craneLifts whole sections clear of structures
  • Bucket truckReach for trimming and take-downs
  • Bobcat track loaderLot clearing, grading the mess away
  • Debris haulingBrush, logs and stumps — gone
What we do

Seven ways we clear the way.

From a single dead limb over the driveway to a whole lot cleared to the dirt — one call, one crew, our own equipment.

01

Tree removal

Dead, dangerous or in the way. Taken down whole or in pieces, hauled off, ground flat if you want it.

02

Tree trimming

Limbs off the roof, deadwood out of the canopy, the shape of the tree kept.

03

Crane service

Lifts over houses, lines and water where a tree can't be dropped.

04

Stump grinding

Stumps ground below grade so you can seed, sod or build over them.

05

Lot clearing

Trees, brush and stumps cleared for building, views or pasture.

06

Debris hauling

Logs, brush and storm debris loaded and hauled away. Nothing left at the curb.

07

Bobcat service

Compact track loader for moving logs, pushing brush and cleaning up the site when the trees are down.
