02 — Ice storm damage

Ice is the one
that takes
Oklahoma trees.

Wind a tree can usually stand. Ice is different. It loads a crown until limbs that held through every summer storm let go in the dark — over a roof, across a drive, into a fence line.

Ice storm damage is one of the five things we do, and the phone is open 24 hours. It is the same number that's painted on the side of the truck.
