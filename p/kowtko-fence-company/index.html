Four materials, one crew

Pick the fence. We'll set the posts.

Wood for privacy, aluminum for a pool that still looks open, chain link when the budget and the dog both matter, vinyl when you never want to stain anything again. Every photo on this page is a Kowtko fence.

01
Wood fences

Privacy you can lean on.

Full-height privacy runs, double gates wide enough for the mower, and clean corners where the fence meets the house or the shed. Fresh-cut boards that will weather to silver — or take a stain when you're ready.

  • Privacy fences around pools, yards and back lots
  • Walk gates and double drive gates
  • Runs that follow the tree line or the property line
Vinyl, too. Same layouts in vinyl for a fence that never needs paint or stain — ask when you call.
02
Aluminum fences

Around the pool without closing it in.

Black aluminum pickets keep the yard open to look at and closed to wander into. It's the fence in most of our pool photos for a reason: it disappears against the trees and it doesn't rust.

  • Pool and patio enclosures
  • Front-yard and courtyard runs
  • Matching gates
03
Chain link fences

The hard-working fence.

Black chain link that fades into the yard instead of shining like a ball field. Good for dogs, kids, pools and big back lots where a privacy fence would cost more than it's worth.

  • Back yards, pools and sheds
  • Tie-ins to existing wood or aluminum fence
  • Gates sized for the mower or the truck
Beyond the fence line

Handrails and repairs.

Two more things on the Kowtko list: custom handrails for steps, porches and walkways, and repairs to fences we didn't build — storm-leaned posts, broken pickets, gates that won't latch.
