01 — The climber

Owner, operator, and the expert tree climber.

Kendal Ellis runs Texoma Tree Service Pros LLC as owner/operator — and describes himself, in his own listing, in three words: expert tree climber.

Look at the job photographs he publishes and every one says the same thing. A rope. A harness. A helmet. A saw. And a man a long way off the ground.

02 — Close quarters

A roof under it. A drive beside it. A line through it.

Close quarters is the hard half of this trade, and it is the thing Texoma Tree Service Pros names first — trees of all sizes, removed safely and efficiently where there is no room to drop them.

In their photographs the answer is the same every time. The tree comes apart at the top, in sections, on a rope — not all at once, and not on your garage.

03 — Line clearance

Beside the power lines.

Line clearance is one of the four services Texoma Tree Service Pros lists — and one of their own photographs shows precisely what it means. Hi-vis shirt. Roped in. A limb coming off with the conductors running straight through the frame.

It is the work that separates a climber from everybody else with a chainsaw: nowhere to drop it, nothing to hit it with, and no margin.
