Client Agreement

Last updated September 20, 2026

This agreement is between Pickr Media ("Pickr Media", "we") and the business that buys a plan ("you"). You accept it when you complete checkout. It's written to be read.

1. What you're buying

A website based on the preview you approved, put live on your domain, plus hosting and updates for as long as your plan is active.

  • Pay monthly: no build fee; a monthly fee that includes the site, hosting and updates. After 12 consecutive paid months, ownership of the site's design and content passes to you.
  • Own it: a one-time build fee, after which the site's design and content are yours, plus a monthly fee for hosting and updates.

The prices are the ones shown at checkout. We'll give at least 30 days' notice by email before any price change, and you can cancel before it takes effect.

2. Updates that are included

Changes to text, hours, phone numbers, photos, service areas, and adding or removing a service, requested by email. We aim to complete them within 2 business days. New functionality (for example online booking, a store, extra page types or a redesign) is outside the plan; we'll quote it and only start once you agree.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Everything on your site must be true. You confirm the business details, claims, licenses, reviews and photos on your site are accurate and that you have the right to use them, including your logo and any images you send us.
  • You're responsible for your own legal and licensing obligations as a business, including anything your trade requires you to display.
  • Tell us promptly if anything on the site becomes out of date.

We build from what your business has already published and from what you tell us. We don't add claims you haven't made.

4. Your domain

Your domain should be registered in your name so you always control it. We'll give you plain instructions for pointing it at your site. We never need your registrar password.

5. Payment

Payments are taken by card through Stripe. Monthly fees are billed in advance. If a payment fails, we'll email you; if it isn't resolved within 10 days, we may take the site offline until it is. Fees already paid are non-refundable, except that if we can't put your approved site live for a reason that's our fault, we'll refund the most recent payment.

6. Cancelling

  • You can cancel anytime by email. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you've already paid for.
  • Pay monthly (before 12 paid months): the site comes down when the plan ends.
  • Own it, or Pay monthly after 12 paid months: ask and we'll send you your site files so you can host them elsewhere.
  • We may end the agreement with 30 days' notice, or immediately if the site is used for anything unlawful, deceptive or abusive.

7. Hosting

We use reputable third-party infrastructure and aim for the site to be available around the clock, but no host can promise zero downtime and we don't. We keep the source of your site so it can be rebuilt and redeployed.

8. What we don't promise

We don't guarantee search rankings, leads, calls or revenue. Search engines make their own decisions.

9. Limit of liability

To the fullest extent the law allows, neither party is liable to the other for indirect or consequential losses, lost profits or lost business. Pickr Media's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees you paid in the three months before the claim arose.

10. If someone makes a claim

If a third party makes a claim against us because of content you supplied or confirmed (for example a photo you didn't have rights to, or a statement about your business that wasn't true), you agree to cover the reasonable costs of that claim.

11. Showing our work

We may show your site as an example of our work. If you'd rather we didn't, tell us and we won't.

12. General

This agreement, the checkout page and our Terms are the whole agreement between us. It is governed by the laws of the United States and of the state in which Pickr Media is based. If part of it can't be enforced, the rest still applies. We communicate by email, and notices sent to the email address on your account count as delivered.
